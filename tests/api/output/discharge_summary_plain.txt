# Discharge Summary (Plain Text)

## ADMISSION SUMMARY

Dear Doctor, Thank you for your ongoing care of patient, a 58-year-old female who presented to the Emergency Department at our hospital on today's date with a 2-month history of knee pain.

Her primary complaint was acute onset knee pain during a yoga session where her knee and hip were flexed. She described the pain as feeling "broken", 10/10 in severity, non-migratory, and making it difficult to walk. Initial investigations included a knee X-ray which showed no findings, and she was provided with crutches.

Currently, her pain is low, though still present with walking. She has been managing her pain with ibuprofen. Her past medical history is notable for chronic wrist pain treated with two steroid injections. She is perimenopausal and takes estrogen replacement, along with turmeric, glucosamine, and evening primrose oil supplements. Given her improved pain and stable condition, she is suitable for discharge.
